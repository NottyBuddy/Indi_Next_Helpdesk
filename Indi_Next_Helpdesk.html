✅ Golden Rules — Every Connected Call
Treat every call like a helpful consultation, not a rigid questionnaire. Every connected call must end with a clear, agreed next action.

Do

  • Ask one question at a time and let the customer respond before continuing
  • Listen actively, acknowledge concerns, keep tone supportive
  • Keep explanations simple — avoid unnecessary technical terms
  • Reconfirm requirement + agreed action, team, date, and time before ending
  • Document every call in the CRM activity, every time

Don't

  • Don't criticize competitors or local fabricators — redirect to IndiFrame's value
  • Don't promise unauthorized discounts, exact delivery dates, or technical specs (wind-load, mm-level, chamber counts) that need technical validation
  • Don't calculate custom prices — quote only "Starts from ₹450/sq ft + GST"
  • Don't confirm installation before a renovation site's existing windows/doors are removed
  • Don't end a call with a vague "our team will contact you" — confirm the full sequence
